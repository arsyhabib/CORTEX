- Quiz and flashcard items are generated from source-derived glossary/section snippets and are labeled with `confidence: inferred_from_source_snippet` or `source_derived`.
- Visual and 3D entries are placeholders for later asset generation/clearance; they are not assertions that such assets exist in the package.

Post-generation surface polish applied: wording-heavy generated fields were rewritten to read more naturally while provenance and reconstruction flags were preserved.
